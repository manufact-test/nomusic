## Reproduced regression

0.4.70 `prepareJoin` permitted 1200 ms for seeking, then required the replacement to be within 300 ms of a projected PRE-SEEK clock. A freshly approved remote MP3 initially has only its first 64 KiB decoded; middle playback can need later sequential ranges. Native playback keeps advancing during this cold seek. Even a successful delayed seek could therefore reject as `replacement-join-not-ready`. Controller destroys the decoder and retries after backoff, beginning again with a cold file. This is a code-level explanation matching the reported repetition, not a claim that the unavailable user trace has been inspected.

Added deterministic regression before changing production code: a completed cold middle seek taking 1800 ms must reuse that decoder and align against a fresh native snapshot. It FAILS on 0.4.70 with `replacement-join-not-ready`.

## Narrow change

Only unguarded mid-song adoption uses the updated join. Preserve decoder and contiguous in-flight ranges while its first seek completes, with a total 15-second join budget. Read a fresh native snapshot after completion; if the original moved ahead, realign the same decoder. At most three completed seeks; do not restart an unfinished seek or restart the asset download just because the native clock advanced. Adoption requires alignment within 200 ms; retain the controller's fresh ownership/duration/eligibility check and final 300 ms guard-admission fence.

Controller supplies a snapshot callback with exact operation ownership and eligibility checks. Stop, manual Original, changed source/track, access suspension and destroy still cancel the decoder and prevent stale adoption. Genuine deadline/transport errors remain bounded and fail open. Existing signed URL, account authorization, duration and range validation remain required.

No changes to MAIN intro hold, Yandex adapter, sync engine, original guard, first-range size, auth, server, Android, native signing, or stable `feature/api-range`. Version 0.4.71 is an isolated desktop archive for one combined owner check.
