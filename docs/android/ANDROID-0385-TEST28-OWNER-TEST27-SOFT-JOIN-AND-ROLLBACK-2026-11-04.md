## Test.28 exact change and bounded hypothesis

1. Test.27 networking, lookahead cache, audio identity, source Media3 observer and all permission rules retained unchanged.
2. In `ReplacementEngine.fade(...)`, **initial exclusive cold join only (both cached local files and fresh sparse streams)**, once the original 100ms transport drift gate passes and the decoder advances: `AudioOwnership.duckSourceForExclusiveJoin` attenuates *only* native Yandex DSP for 32ms while replacement volume stays zero; then `grantReplacement` and decoder volume ramps 0→full in 48ms. No simultaneous positive source and decoder gain. No 500ms valley, no independent observer, no new Android permissions. It is an unmeasured psychoacoustic hypothesis, not a claim that original and replacement PCM are phase-identical.
3. On fast seek/resume of an already playing song, `shapeExclusiveCold=false`: **immediate original mute/decoder grant** retained to avoid new finger lag. Media3 pause missing at source is still a recognized, uncorrected upstream limitation; the safe engine reacts when it truly receives `source.playing=false` and avoids inventing pause authority.
4. Host replay verifies initial mute, no overlap, finishing solely on replacement and **source gain restoration when paused in the middle of a partial cold duck**. Original seek()/completeSeek() code and golden HONOR checks unchanged. Never claim speaker-level acoustic verification from host/CI.
