[Queue role CI #44097317189 SUCCESS](https://github.com/manufact-test/nomusic/actions/runs/44097317189), [register microtrace CI #44097745386 SUCCESS](https://github.com/manufact-test/nomusic/actions/runs/44097745386).
2. **Exact front-advance structure.** \`h.if()Lxh6;\` is **not** a simple getter. Its signed bytecode reads \`SLOT_A\` at PC 49, reads an \`Lxh6\`-typed successor field on that same holder at PC 51, stores the successor in the *same exact* \`SLOT_A\` field at PC 53, then re-reads and returns \`SLOT_A\` at PCs 58/60. Conditional adjustments to \`SLOT_B\` and \`SLOT_C\` use the same successor DEX field reference. The exact field/register/return contract is guarded fail-closed. This proves a **front/advance role**, not audible active playback. \`h.this()Lxh6;\` separately returns \`SLOT_C\` (PC 0/2). \`SLOT_D\` is seen on the new-holder tail-admission path; do not infer a live playing role from that alone. - The static comparison is **not** a *runtime equality observation*. Neither a front/reading/loaded queue position nor a field reference can be promoted to "currently audible" without observing which \`Lxh6\`/stream is actually attached and being consumed during owner playback. \`SLOT_A\` has a front-advance contract; \`SLOT_B\` is passed to renderer-stream checks; their actual live meaning and timing require verification.
