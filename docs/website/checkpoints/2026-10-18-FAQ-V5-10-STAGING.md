## Symptom and root cause

The opening FAQ animation in v5.8 finished with an extra 23px desktop / 19px mobile increase in card height. Approved CSS uses global `box-sizing:border-box`, but the WAAPI target had been calculated as `scrollHeight - paddingBottom`: content-box height rather than the true final border-box height. At animation cleanup, restoring CSS padding caused a visible second step.

**Fix:** `website/public/assets/js/v10/owner-v10-motion.js` now measures the rendered natural `.answer` border-box via `getBoundingClientRect().height` after restoring natural height and padding only during a synchronous, off-frame measurement. Both height and bottom padding animate to final values together; cleanup does not add another 23/19px. Closing/reopen and interruptions retain the current visual frame.

**Global exclusivity:** one FAQ controller manages all 28 native `details/summary` entries, across both grid columns and categories. Switching to another item queues its opening until the previous item has **finished closing** and `details.open` was removed. Thus no two questions remain `open` at once, even during transitions. Rapid reversals, pending target replacement, category filters, direct hashes, and reduced motion are supported. Native `details/summary` and keyboard activation remain intact.
